Most watchlists dump live prices and leave you to decide what matters. MarketPulse answers one question: what changed since you last looked, and why should you care? When you mark the list as checked, we store a baseline of price, volume, news, sentiment, and volatility. Next visit, a deterministic engine diffs live US data against that snapshot, scores 0–100, and ranks only stocks whose signals combine into something meaningful. Isolated noise stays quiet; price plus volume plus news is high-impact, with a why and a driver breakdown. Built as a working Next.js + Postgres app with Finnhub quotes — not mock screens.
